About FleetBuilt Partners

Launch support for an employer-owned training path

FleetBuilt Partners helps fleets map and stand up an internal CDL academy. You supply the trucks. You remain the regulated training provider.

Our approach

A build path when hiring stays expensive

Fleets already know the cost of an empty seat, an outside school calendar, and a truck that sits. Searching the market is one option. Building an inside path is another.

FleetBuilt Partners provides launch support — a Feasibility Blueprint, then founding implementation if you decide the same scoped project is worth contracting. We do not operate your CDL school.

Our logo is a truck with a green graduation cap — a mark for the training path you want to build. It does not mean we operate your CDL school.

What we hold to

  • You remain the provider
  • One site and one class for founding work
  • Locked $2,500 / $15,000 fees
  • Blueprint credit within 60 days
  • Third-party costs additional
  • No approval or hiring-result promises
